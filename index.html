About
Hi, I'm Talha — an EEE student at Bilkent University working at the intersection of robot learning, computer vision, and optimization. I'm interested in how robots can perceive 3D scenes, reason about objects and tools, and plan meaningful physical actions in the real world.

Most of my work touches on robotic manipulation, affordance reasoning, and world models. Right now I'm doing research on affordance-guided world models for dexterous manipulation at NYU CILVR with Lerrel Pinto, and contributing to CVXPY at Stanford on disciplined nonlinear programming.

I've studied as a visiting student at UC Berkeley and Stanford, taking courses in machine learning, convex optimization, information devices and systems, and technology leadership. Outside of coursework, I like building projects that tie together perception, planning, and control — with the long-term goal of making robotic systems more capable and reliable.
